# Notion backlinks: where they show, and why they sometimes don't

Backlinks are **the list of other pages that point at the page you are on**, and they sit just above the page title as "N backlinks". On a database page they appear with the properties. There is nothing to switch on: the moment another page @-mentions or links to this one, the backlink appears.

If you are not seeing one, it is usually one of three things.

- **Nothing links here yet.** A subpage, or a page sitting in the same database, is membership rather than a link, so it does not count as a backlink
- **The tie is a relation property.** A relation is a property, not a backlink. That connection shows in the property, not in the backlink list
- **Permissions.** A link from a page you cannot access does not appear in your list. A link from a page only you can see shows to others as `Private`

That part is Notion's job and it works. The limit is elsewhere. Backlinks are **one page at a time**. They show what points here, not where those pages lead next, and not what the whole looks like; for that you open pages one by one and stitch it together in your head. Membership and relations are outside the list from the start.

Notion-mind draws all three on one screen. Subpages and database membership are solid lines, relation properties are dotted, and hovering a node keeps only the connections in and out of that page while the rest dims. It is the backlink panel, read as a map instead of one page at a time.

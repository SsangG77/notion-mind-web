## 2. Personal Data We Collect

### Account and identification data
Items: Notion user ID, Notion workspace ID, workspace name
Source: Notion OAuth, when you sign in

### Authentication data
Items: Notion access token (encrypted, stored in a browser cookie)
Source: Notion OAuth

### Notion workspace content
Items: page titles, database properties, relations, and short body previews of the pages you connected
Source: Notion API, read on your request. This content is streamed to your browser for display and is not stored on our servers.

### Subscription data (Pro subscribers only)
Items: payment-provider customer ID, subscription ID, subscription status
Source: Paddle, our payment provider

### Usage data
Items: IP address, browser and device information, access timestamps, cookies
Source: automatic, when you use the Service

### Payment data
Your name, email address, card details, and billing address entered at checkout are collected and processed directly by Paddle.com Market Ltd, which acts as Merchant of Record and independent controller for the transaction. We never receive or store your card details.

We do not process any special categories of personal data (Article 9).

## 3. Purposes and Legal Bases (Article 6)

### Providing the Service (sign-in, reading and visualising your workspace)
Legal basis: Contract (Art. 6(1)(b))

### Managing Pro subscriptions, billing, and refunds
Legal basis: Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)); Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) for tax and accounting records

### Security, fraud and abuse prevention, error diagnosis
Legal basis: Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f))

Legal basis: Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), given through the cookie banner. You can withdraw consent at any time.

### Showing non-personalised advertising to Free users
Legal basis: Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f))
Specific legitimate interest: funding the free tier of the Service

Providing account and authentication data is necessary to use the Service; without it we cannot display your workspace. Providing consent to advertising cookies is optional.

## 6. Retention

- **Account and authentication data**: deleted from your browser immediately on logout, or 30 days after issue if you do not log out. Criteria: session lifetime.
- **Subscription status and user settings**: 30 days after the subscription ends or the workspace is disconnected. Criteria: contract end.
- **Billing and transaction records**: 5 years. Criteria: Korean e-commerce and tax law retention periods.
- **Access logs**: up to 1 year. Criteria: security and legal requirements.
- **Advertising cookies**: up to 13 months. Criteria: provider policy and your consent.

After the retention period, data is deleted or anonymized.

## 7. Your Rights Under GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- **Right of Access (Art. 15)** — obtain a copy of your data and information about processing
- **Right to Rectification (Art. 16)** — correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Right to Erasure (Art. 17)** — request deletion ("right to be forgotten") in certain circumstances
- **Right to Restriction (Art. 18)** — restrict processing in certain circumstances
- **Right to Data Portability (Art. 20)** — receive your data in structured, machine-readable format
- **Right to Object (Art. 21)** — object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing
- **Right Not to Be Subject to Automated Decision-Making (Art. 22)** — including profiling with legal or similarly significant effects. We do not make such decisions.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3))** — where consent is the legal basis, withdraw at any time via the cookie settings link on this page

To exercise any right, contact us at sangjincha719@gmail.com. We will respond within **one month** (extendable by two months for complex requests, with notice). You can also disconnect Notion-mind at any time under "Connected apps" in your Notion settings, which revokes our access to your workspace.

## 8. Right to Lodge a Complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority. A list is available at: https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en

## 9. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies. Strictly necessary cookies (sign-in session, security, your theme and cookie preferences) are always set. Advertising cookies (Google AdSense) are used only with your prior opt-in consent. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked through Google's certified consent management platform (IAB TCF), which lets you consent, refuse, or manage options and can be reopened from the ad settings link Google shows. "Reject" is as easy as "Accept".

Details of Google's advertising cookies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Opt out of personalised ads: https://adssettings.google.com.

## 10. Automated Decision-Making (Article 22)

We do not use automated decision-making, including profiling, that produces legal effects or similarly significantly affects you.

## 12. Security
